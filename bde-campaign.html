The Research

Five revenue leaks silently draining practices like yours

This is what industry data says is happening right now — not to hospitals with billing departments, but to independent practices doing their best with a front desk and hope.

1. Denials written off, never appealed

Practices appeal less than half of denied claims. The rest are quietly adjusted off — pure revenue gone.

Typical loss: 3–5% of monthly collections

2. Underpayments nobody checks

Payers short-pay claims all the time. If nobody compares payment vs. contract rate, the difference never comes back.

Typical loss: 2–4% of collections

3. Aged claims aging out

Claims over 90 days rarely get worked, and past payer timely-filing limits they're uncollectable — legally.

Typical loss: 1–3% of collections

4. Prior authorization chaos

12 hours a week of staff time chasing auths — time taken straight away from patients at the front desk.

Cost: staff burnout + delayed care

5. Eligibility surprises

Wrong coverage details at check-in become denial + rework + an awkward patient conversation 30 days later.

Cost: denials + patient trust

Verified eligibility up front, claims submitted within 24 hours, every denial followed within 48, payments reconciled against contract rates. Every month. That's it.
