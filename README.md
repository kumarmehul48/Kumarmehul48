## What I Do — 4 Pillars

### 🩺 1 · For US Healthcare Providers
**Revenue Cycle Management — So You Can Focus on Patients**

You didn't become a physician to chase claims. But here you are — stuck at 6:40 PM on a Tuesday, staring at a denied claim, while patients wait for follow-ups.

I take the entire revenue cycle off your plate:

- **End-to-End RCM** — eligibility, coding, submission, posting, reconciliation
- **Denial Management** — every denial analyzed, appealed, and prevented at the root
- **A/R Recovery** — weekly work cycles, before the 90-day cliff
- **Underpayment Recovery** — payment vs. contract reconciliation
- **Eligibility & Credentialing** — verification before every visit
- **HIPAA-Compliant Operations** — full compliance, secure data handling
- **Documented Systems** — SOPs that survive staff turnover
- **Revenue Cycle Audits** — clear findings, prioritized action plan

**The SLA I hold myself to:**

| Element | Commitment |
|:---|:---|
| Claim Submission | Within **24 hours** of visit |
| Denial Follow-Up | Within **48 hours** of receipt |
| A/R Recovery | Worked **every week** |
| Reporting | **Monthly**, in plain English |
| Point of Contact | **Me — directly** |

**No billing headache. No claim nightmare. Just clean revenue — and your time back.**
