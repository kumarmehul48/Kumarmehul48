## The Problem I Solve for US Practices

Most US practices lose **10–20% of collectible revenue** every year — invisibly.

It's not one dramatic event. It's small, ongoing leaks nobody is paid to watch:

| Leak | What's Happening |
|:---|:---|
| **01 — Denials** | Up to 90% are recoverable. Most practices never resubmit them. |
| **02 — Underpayments** | Payers pay less than contracted rates. Nobody checks. |
| **03 — Aged A/R** | After 90 days, collectability collapses. Every day counts. |
| **04 — Eligibility Gaps** | Claims denied before submission. Pure preventable loss. |
| **05 — No System** | ~30% billing-staff turnover = your revenue restarts from zero. |

**If two or more feel familiar, your practice is leaking money right now.**

**[Fix the leak →](https://kumarmehul48.github.io/Kumarmehul48/services.html#healthcare)**
