Sound Familiar, Doctor?

These aren't abstract industry problems. They're the six things I hear from every practice owner I talk to — and quietly fix.

PAIN 01

"Denials come back and just get written off"

Up to 90% of denials are recoverable — but most practices never resubmit. Each denied claim also costs $25+ to rework, so staff avoids the pile. The money stays lost.

PAIN 02

"I'm doing prior auths at 9 pm"

Physicians complete 39–45 PAs per week, eating 13+ hours. 88% say it delays patient care. It's the #1 burnout driver after documentation.

PAIN 03

"Payers short me and I can't tell"

Underpayments never bounce a claim — they just pay less than contracted. Nobody audits 835s against your fee schedules. It's invisible loss, every single check.

PAIN 04

"My A/R is a graveyard after 90 days"

After 90 days, collectability collapses. Commercial collection rates have dropped to 34–48%. Old claims sit, staff focuses on new ones, and the cycle repeats.

PAIN 05

"My biller quit and took the process with her"

~30% annual billing-staff turnover. When your biller leaves, your revenue engine resets to zero while you hire, train, and bleed during the gap.

PAIN 06

"I don't actually know what I'm owed"

No clean monthly numbers: collections, denials, aging, per-payer. You find out about a bad month two months late — from your bank balance, not a report.

If two or more of these feel like your practice — we should talk this week, not someday.
