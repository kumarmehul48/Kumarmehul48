1. Overview & Our Commitment

At Mehul Parmar ("I," "me," "my," or "the business"), I aim to deliver honest, high-quality professional services. This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains when refunds are available, how cancellations work, and what you can expect if something doesn't go as planned.

My goal is fairness on both sides:

  • You should never pay for work that wasn't delivered
  • Work already completed must be paid for
  • Refunds should be simple, transparent, and quick to process when legitimate

2. General Principles

  • All refund requests must be made in writing by email to kumarmehul48@gmail.com
  • Refunds are issued in the original payment method whenever possible
  • Refund eligibility depends on service type, stage of delivery, and scope agreement
  • Where a signed Statement of Work (SOW) or service agreement exists, that document's refund terms take precedence
  • All times quoted below are in business days unless stated otherwise

3. RCM & Medical Billing Services

Because RCM engagements are performed over time and often involve ongoing work on your behalf, refunds are handled as follows:

3.1 Setup / Onboarding Fees

  • Full refund if cancelled before onboarding work begins
  • Pro-rated refund if cancelled after onboarding starts but before claims are submitted
  • No refund once claims have been submitted or A/R work has begun

3.2 Monthly Retainers

  • Refunds are not provided for months where work has been performed
  • If cancelled mid-month, no refund for the current month; the following month is not billed
  • 30-day written notice is required to cancel ongoing retainers, unless otherwise agreed

3.3 Percentage-of-Collections Engagements

  • Fees are earned only on collections received — no refunds apply on collected amounts
  • If engagement is terminated, fees on collections received after termination date are still payable per the signed agreement

3.4 Audit & Consultation Fees

  • Full refund if cancelled 72 hours or more before the scheduled audit or consultation
  • 50% refund if cancelled between 24–72 hours before
  • No refund if cancelled less than 24 hours before, or after delivery has begun

4. Business Consulting Services

This covers website builds, brand positioning, digital marketing, and online presence projects.

4.1 Project-Based Work (e.g., Website Builds)

  • Full refund of deposit if cancelled before any work has started
  • 50% refund of deposit if cancelled after discovery/planning but before development begins
  • No refund of deposit once development or execution work has begun
  • Milestone payments already invoiced and delivered are non-refundable

4.2 Monthly Retainers (Marketing / Support)

  • No refund for months where work has been performed
  • Cancellation requires 30-day written notice
  • Partial months are not pro-rated unless agreed in writing

4.3 Third-Party Costs

  • Any third-party costs (domains, hosting, ad spend, subscription tools) are non-refundable by us — refunds for these must be claimed directly from the third-party vendor

5. Career Mentorship Sessions

Career mentorship sessions are 1:1 time with you — so scheduling matters.

5.1 Single Sessions

  • Full refund if cancelled 48 hours or more before the session
  • 50% refund if cancelled between 24–48 hours before
  • No refund if cancelled less than 24 hours before, or for no-shows
  • Free rescheduling once with at least 24 hours notice

5.2 Multi-Session Packages

  • Unused sessions can be refunded at a pro-rated rate, minus any discount received on the package
  • Used sessions are non-refundable
  • Packages expire 6 months from purchase date unless otherwise agreed

6. AI & Skills Mentorship

6.1 Live Sessions (1:1 or Group)

  • Full refund if cancelled 48 hours or more before the session
  • 50% refund if cancelled between 24–48 hours before
  • No refund for no-shows or cancellations under 24 hours

6.2 Recorded Courses / Digital Content

  • Due to the digital nature and instant access, no refunds are provided once content has been accessed, downloaded, or viewed
  • If access has not been used, a refund may be considered within 7 days of purchase — at our discretion

6.3 Cohort Programs

  • Refunds are available if requested before the cohort start date
  • Once a cohort has begun, no refunds are provided

7. Cancellation Policy

To cancel any engagement, session, or subscription:

  • Send a written cancellation request to kumarmehul48@gmail.com
  • Include your name, engagement details, and effective cancellation date
  • You will receive an acknowledgement within 2 business days
  • Any work in progress will be stopped once cancellation is acknowledged
  • Completed work remains payable; refund terms apply as per Sections 3–6

8. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, follow these steps:

  • Email kumarmehul48@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request"
  • Include: your full name, invoice number (if applicable), service purchased, reason for the request, and the amount requested
  • If the reason relates to a technical or delivery issue, provide supporting details or screenshots
  • You will receive an acknowledgement within 2 business days
  • A decision will be communicated within 5–7 business days of acknowledgement

  • Processing typically takes 5–10 business days from approval
  • Bank, payment gateway, or currency conversion delays may extend this beyond our control
  • Any transaction or currency conversion fees charged by payment providers are non-refundable

10. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be provided in the following cases:

  • Work that has already been completed and delivered
  • Change of mind after deliverables are in progress
  • Digital content that has been accessed, downloaded, or viewed
  • Third-party costs (domains, hosting, ad spend, subscriptions)
  • Client-caused delays or failures (e.g., not providing required information)
  • Cancellations made after the specified notice period
  • Requests based on dissatisfaction with results that were never guaranteed (see Disclaimer)
  • Any engagement terminated due to Client breach of Terms of Service

11. Rights Under Indian Law

For clients in India, nothing in this Refund Policy overrides your statutory rights under:

  • The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 — including the right to seek redressal for deficient services
  • The Indian Contract Act, 1872
  • Applicable GST and invoice regulations

If you believe a service was deficient and this Policy does not fairly resolve it, you may approach the appropriate Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

12. Rights Under US Law

For clients in the United States:

  • State-specific consumer protection laws may provide additional refund rights
  • If a signed service agreement specifies governing law, that law applies to refund disputes
  • For HIPAA-covered engagements, refund terms are governed by the signed service agreement and Business Associate Agreement
  • Nothing in this Policy limits any rights you may have under applicable federal or state law

13. Dispute Resolution

If you are unhappy with a refund decision:

  • Reply to the decision email with additional context or documentation
  • We will review and respond within 5 business days
  • If unresolved, the matter may be escalated under the Terms of Service dispute resolution process (Section 15)
  • For Indian consumers, statutory remedies under the Consumer Protection Act remain available

Our promise: If a refund is genuinely owed under this Policy, we will process it without argument. If it is not, we will explain why clearly and respectfully.
