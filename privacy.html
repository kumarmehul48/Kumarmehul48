1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Mehul Parmar ("I," "me," "my," or "the business") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you visit kumarmehul48.github.io or use any of my professional services.

I am committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data with transparency, integrity, and respect. This Policy reflects the requirements of:

  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) — India
  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — European Union and United Kingdom
  • The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) / CPRA — California, USA
  • The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) — for healthcare-related engagements in the USA
  • General best practices for global privacy and data protection

2. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to:

  • Information collected through this website
  • Information you provide when booking a call, sending a message, or engaging my services
  • Information collected during delivery of Revenue Cycle Management (RCM), medical billing support, business consulting, career mentorship, or AI training services
  • Any other communication, form submission, or interaction with my business

It does not apply to third-party websites or services you may reach through links on this site.

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, and business details

  • Device and browser data: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system

Important: This website does not knowingly collect Protected Health Information (PHI) through public forms. If you are a US healthcare provider engaging RCM services, PHI handling is governed by a separate Business Associate Agreement (BAA) executed directly with your practice.

Your information is used only for legitimate business purposes, including:

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR & DPDP Act)

5.1 Under GDPR (EU/UK)

We process personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: When you submit a form, book a call, or opt into communication
  • Contract: When processing is required to deliver a service you've engaged
  • Legitimate interest: For business operations, service improvement, and professional outreach
  • Legal obligation: For tax, accounting, and compliance requirements

5.2 Under India's DPDP Act, 2023

We process personal data as a Data Fiduciary based on:

  • Your consent provided at the time of collection (which you may withdraw anytime)
  • Legitimate uses permitted under the Act (e.g., employment, legal compliance, medical emergency, public interest)
  • Purpose limitation and data minimisation principles

6. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We share your information only in limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted third parties who help run the business (e.g., Calendly, email providers, hosting). They are contractually bound to protect your data
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or regulatory authority
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you
  • With your consent: When you explicitly ask us to share information

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by law:

  • Enquiry data: Up to 24 months after last contact

  • Website analytics: Aggregated only; not linked to individuals

After the retention period, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/SSL)
  • Access controls and authentication for internal systems

  • Secure communication channels (WhatsApp Business, encrypted email where available)

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. HIPAA & Protected Health Information (PHI)

For US healthcare providers engaging Revenue Cycle Management, medical billing, denial management, or related services, HIPAA compliance is a core requirement of every engagement.

  • All PHI handling follows HIPAA Privacy, Security, and Breach Notification Rules
  • A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is executed before any PHI is accessed or processed
  • PHI is transmitted only through secure, encrypted channels
  • Access to PHI is limited to personnel directly involved in delivering the service
  • PHI is never used for marketing, resale, or purposes outside the agreed engagement

Any PHI shared outside these secure channels is done at your own risk and is not recommended.

11. Your Rights (General)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to deletion — request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Right to withdraw consent — at any time, where processing is consent-based
  • Right to object — to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to complain — to your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email kumarmehul48@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by your jurisdiction.

12. Rights Under India's DPDP Act, 2023

If you are a Data Principal in India, you have the following rights under the DPDP Act:

  • Right to access information about the personal data being processed
  • Right to correction and erasure of personal data
  • Right to grievance redressal — contact the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved
  • Right to nominate — nominate another individual to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity
  • Right to withdraw consent — with the same ease as providing it

We act as a Data Fiduciary and will process your request in line with the DPDP Act's requirements.

13. Rights Under CCPA / CPRA (California)

California residents have the following rights under the CCPA/CPRA:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected, used, or shared
  • Right to delete personal information, subject to legal exceptions
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — we do not sell or share personal information
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To exercise California rights, email kumarmehul48@gmail.com with the subject line "California Privacy Request."

14. Rights Under GDPR (EU / UK)

If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have rights under the GDPR, including:

  • Restriction of and objection to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time
  • Lodging a complaint with your local supervisory authority

Legal bases for processing are outlined in Section 5. Our lawful basis for most processing is consent or legitimate interest.
